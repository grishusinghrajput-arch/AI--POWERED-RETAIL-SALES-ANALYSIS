# 📊 AI-Powered Retail Sales Analysis | Excel • SQL • Power BI • ChatGPT

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing 2,000 retail sales records to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, understand product profitability, and generate actionable business insights.

With the assistance of ChatGPT, Excel, SQL, and Power BI, the project transforms raw retail data into meaningful analysis and interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **ChatGPT:** AI-assisted analysis, SQL query support, and analytical problem-solving.
* **Excel:** Data cleaning, data quality checks, and data preparation.
* **SQL (MySQL):** Data querying, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs, CASE statements, and window functions.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI monitoring, and business data visualization.

## ❓ Business Problems

* What are the company's total sales and profit?
* Which region generates the highest sales?
* Which product categories contribute the most profit?
* What are the top 5 revenue-generating products?
* Which regions have the strongest sales performance?
* Which orders perform above the overall average sales?
* Which products generate high sales but relatively low profit?
* How do cumulative sales change over time?

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Total Sales:** ₹2.08 Crore
* **Total Profit:** ₹27.95 Lakh
* **Total Records:** 2,000
* **Top Sales Region:** South
* **Top Revenue Product:** Laptop
* **Most Profitable Category:** Electronics
* Identified products with negative profit for further investigation.
* Analyzed regional performance and cumulative sales trends.
